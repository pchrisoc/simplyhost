SimplyHost

Self-hosting should be approachable.

SimplyHost is an open-source project focused on making self-hosting easier to understand, plan, and deploy. It is designed to help anyone build and manage their own home server without requiring years of systems administration experience.

Choose what you want to host, understand what resources it needs, and get clear guidance for building a system that works.
