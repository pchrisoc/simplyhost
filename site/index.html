SimplyHost

Self-hosting for everyone.

Your photos, files, music, and everyday tools deserve a home you control. SimplyHost is an open-source project being built to make running your own server approachable, whether you’re starting with a spare computer or building a dedicated homelab.

Start with your hardware, choose the apps you want, and get a clear plan for setting them up. Optional Budget Mode will help you see how each choice fits your available memory, processing power, and storage.

SimplyHost will bring together virtual machines, applications, networking, remote access, and backups in one guided workflow. A modular catalog will track upstream releases to help keep your setup current as projects evolve.

Built around your hardware. Guided at your pace. Yours to understand, change, and maintain.

SimplyHost · Open source. Built for everyone.
